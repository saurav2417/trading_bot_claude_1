## Full truck or part load — you choose

- **Full Truck Load:** your goods travel alone, direct, fastest option. Best above ~3–4 tonnes or for
  time-critical/fragile cargo.
- **Part Load:** pay only for the space you use. We consolidate with other Siliguri-bound consignments —
  the economical choice for smaller shipments.
