## What makes hill transport different

- **Vehicle size limits:** NH10 and inner Sikkim roads restrict large trailers. We transship to
  right-sized vehicles at Siliguri when needed — seamlessly, within the quoted price.
- **Weather planning:** monsoon landslides on NH10 are a real factor June–September. We track route status
  daily and quote honestly rather than promising impossible dates.
- **Permits & documentation:** interstate e-way bills and Sikkim entry formalities handled with the consignment.
