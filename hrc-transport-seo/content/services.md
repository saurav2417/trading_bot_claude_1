# Our Transport Services

From a single carton to a dedicated 22-ft truck, HRC Transport covers commercial cargo movement across
Kolkata, North Bengal, Sikkim and Bhutan. Every service below comes with committed timelines, transparent
pricing and GST documentation.

## Full Truck Load (FTL)
A dedicated vehicle for your consignment — direct, fastest, and safest for large or sensitive cargo.
Vehicles from Tata 407 up to 22-ft containers. Best for loads above ~3–4 tonnes or time-critical dispatches.

## Part Load (PTL)
Pay only for the space you use. We consolidate consignments on our daily
[Kolkata–Siliguri](/kolkata-to-siliguri-transport/) corridor and onward routes, making smaller shipments
economical without long waiting times.

## Medical & Surgical Equipment Transport
Hospitals, distributors and diagnostics suppliers trust us with sensitive medical cargo — careful loading,
minimal handling and on-schedule delivery. *(Our customer reviews specifically mention safe surgical material
delivery — keep those testimonials displayed here.)*

## Commercial & Wholesale Goods
Regular distributor and retailer stock movement — FMCG, hardware, textiles, electronics — with predictable
schedules your supply chain can plan around.

## Machinery & Project Cargo
Industrial machinery, spares and project consignments, including hill-route delivery into Sikkim and
cross-border movement to Bhutan's infrastructure projects.

## Documentation & Compliance
GST invoicing · e-way bill assistance · border documentation guidance for Bhutan consignments ·
proof of delivery on request.
