## The border, handled

India–Bhutan trade moves overwhelmingly through the **Jaigaon–Phuentsholing** land crossing. Delays there are
almost always documentation problems. Because we run this crossing regularly, we tell you **exactly what
paperwork your consignment needs before dispatch** — invoice copies, e-way bill to the border, and the
import formalities on the Bhutan side — so trucks don't sit at the gate.
