# How to Send Goods from India to Bhutan by Road: The Complete 2026 Guide

India is Bhutan's largest trading partner, and almost all of that trade travels one road corridor. If you're a
supplier, exporter or project contractor shipping to Bhutan for the first time, this guide covers the route,
the border, the paperwork and the realistic timelines — from a transporter that runs the corridor regularly.

## The route: one border matters

Nearly all commercial road cargo enters Bhutan through the **Jaigaon (India) – Phuentsholing (Bhutan)**
crossing in Alipurduar district, West Bengal. From Kolkata the corridor runs via Siliguri to Jaigaon —
roughly **700 km, 2–3 days by truck**. Phuentsholing is Bhutan's commercial hub; from there goods move
onward to Thimphu (~170 km of hill road, about a day), Paro and other destinations.

## Documents you'll need

Requirements vary by goods type, but a typical commercial consignment needs:

- **Commercial invoice** — with accurate goods description and value.
- **E-way bill** — for the Indian leg to the border, mandatory above the value threshold.
- **Bhutan import formalities** — handled at Phuentsholing; Bhutanese importers registered with the
  Department of Revenue & Customs clear goods against the invoice. Certain categories (food, pharma,
  plants/animal products) need additional certificates.
- **Transporter documentation** — vehicle papers and, for Indian trucks entering Bhutan, entry permits
  arranged at the border.

**The practical tip that saves days:** border delays are almost always paperwork mismatches — invoice
descriptions that don't match goods, missing e-way bills, or an unregistered importer on the Bhutan side.
An experienced transporter checks the file *before* dispatch. We provide a documentation checklist with
every Bhutan quote.

## What moves on this corridor

Construction materials and hardware for Bhutan's infrastructure and hydel projects · food products and FMCG ·
industrial machinery and spares · medical supplies · retail stock for Phuentsholing and Thimphu markets.

## Costs: what to expect

Bhutan cargo is priced like domestic FTL/PTL freight plus border handling. The main variables are vehicle
size, goods type and final delivery point (border drop vs Thimphu door delivery). Part-load consolidation on
the Kolkata–Siliguri–Jaigaon corridor keeps smaller consignments economical — see how that works on our
[Kolkata to Siliguri service](/kolkata-to-siliguri-transport/).
