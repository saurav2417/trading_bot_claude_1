# Kolkata to Siliguri Transport Charges: What You'll Actually Pay (2026 Guide)

If you've searched for transport charges from Kolkata to Siliguri, you've probably noticed nobody publishes a
simple price. That's not evasiveness — it's because road freight pricing genuinely depends on five factors.
This guide explains them honestly, so you can compare quotes intelligently and avoid the hidden charges that
inflate a "cheap" quote after your goods are loaded.

## The two pricing models

**1. Part Load (PTL) — priced per kg or per unit.**
Your goods share truck space with other consignments heading to Siliguri. You pay for the weight/volume you
occupy. Economical for anything from a few cartons up to ~3 tonnes. The trade-off: consolidated loads may wait
briefly until the truck fills, so transit is quoted in a range.

**2. Full Truck Load (FTL) — priced per vehicle.**
The whole truck is yours: direct pickup to direct delivery, fastest and safest. Pricing depends on the vehicle
you need — a Tata 407 costs far less than a 22-ft container truck. Economical above ~3–4 tonnes, or whenever
timing and handling matter more than squeezing the rate.

## The five factors that move the price

1. **Weight and volume.** Freight charges on whichever is higher — actual weight or volumetric weight. Light
   but bulky goods (furniture, empty containers, insulation) are charged by space, not kilos.
2. **Goods type.** Fragile, high-value or sensitive cargo (electronics, medical equipment) needs careful
   handling and sometimes dedicated vehicles — priced accordingly, and worth it.
3. **Vehicle type.** 407 (~2.5T) → 709 (~4.5T) → 14ft (~7T) → 22ft (~10T+). Matching vehicle to load is where
   an experienced transporter saves you money.
4. **Fuel prices.** Diesel is the largest cost input on a 600 km run; quotes track it.
5. **Season and route conditions.** Festival season demand (Sept–Nov) and monsoon disruptions can tighten
   truck availability on the North Bengal corridor.

## Hidden charges to ask about (before you book)

A trustworthy quote should state clearly whether it includes: loading/unloading labour · door pickup and door
delivery (vs godown-to-godown) · toll and border checkpost costs · waiting/detention charges if loading is
delayed · GST. **Ask for the all-inclusive figure in writing.** At HRC Transport, the rate we agree is the
rate you pay — it's the reason "reasonable charges" appears repeatedly in our customer reviews.

## Typical transit time

Kolkata to Siliguri is roughly 600 km via NH12/NH27. A full truck typically delivers in **1–2 days**;
part loads in **2–3 days** including consolidation. Monsoon months can add a day — a transporter who quotes
honest dates is worth more than one who promises the impossible.
