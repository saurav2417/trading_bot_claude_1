# Full Truck Load vs Part Load: Which Should You Choose?

Every shipper eventually faces this choice: book a whole truck, or pay for space in a shared one? Choose wrong
and you either overpay for empty truck space or wait days for a consolidation that never fills. Here's the
honest breakdown we give our own customers.

## Full Truck Load (FTL) — the dedicated option

The entire vehicle carries only your consignment, driving directly from your pickup to your delivery point.

**Choose FTL when:**
- Your load is above roughly **3–4 tonnes** (the per-kg math starts favouring a dedicated vehicle);
- Timing is critical — no consolidation wait, no intermediate stops;
- Goods are fragile, high-value or sensitive (medical equipment, electronics) — loaded once, unloaded once;
- You need a specific vehicle type (container for weather protection, specific length for machinery).

## Part Load (PTL) — the economical option

Your goods share a truck with other consignments heading the same way; you pay per kg or per carton.

**Choose PTL when:**
- The load is small — a few cartons up to ~3 tonnes;
- Your dates are flexible by a day or two (consolidation adds time);
- The route has regular consolidated departures. This is key: PTL only works well on corridors the
  transporter runs daily — like our [Kolkata–Siliguri service](/kolkata-to-siliguri-transport/).

## The decision table

| Factor | FTL | PTL |
|---|---|---|
| Load size | 3–4T and above | Cartons to ~3T |
| Speed | Fastest, direct | +1–2 days for consolidation |
| Handling | Loaded once | Shared handling |
| Cost for small loads | High (paying for empty space) | **Low** |
| Cost for large loads | **Low per tonne** | Not applicable |
| Fragile/sensitive goods | **Best** | Possible with proper packing |

## Two real-world examples

**A Siliguri distributor restocking 800 kg of FMCG:** PTL, clearly — pays for under a tonne of space on a
daily corridor and receives goods in 2–3 days at a fraction of a dedicated truck's cost.

**A hospital supplier sending surgical equipment to Gangtok:** FTL with a right-sized vehicle — minimal
handling, direct hill delivery on [the Sikkim route](/kolkata-to-gangtok-sikkim-transport/), and a
committed date. The rate difference is small insurance against damage to high-value cargo.

## A hybrid worth knowing about

On long corridors like [Kolkata to Bhutan](/kolkata-to-bhutan-transport/), consolidation to the border hub
plus dedicated final delivery often beats both pure options on price — ask your transporter whether the route
supports it.
