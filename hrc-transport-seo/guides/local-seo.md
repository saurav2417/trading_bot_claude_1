# Local SEO — Google Business Profile & Citations

You already have a GBP (good — that's the hardest part). Now make it work harder.

## 1. One canonical identity (NAP)
Decide the exact string once and use it EVERYWHERE (website footer, GBP, IndiaMART, Justdial, invoices):

> **Name:** HRC Transport
> **Address:** [VERIFY-ADDRESS] (assumed: 21 Phears Lane, Kolkata 700012, West Bengal)
> **Phone:** +91-XXXXX-XXXXX

⚠️ IndiaMART currently lists "HRC Logistics (Hindusthan Road Carriers)". Pick ONE public brand name and edit
the other listings to match. Mixed names split your local ranking signals.

## 2. Complete every GBP field
- **Category (primary):** Transportation service. Secondary: Logistics service, Trucking company, Freight forwarding service.
- **Service area:** add Kolkata, Siliguri, Gangtok, Sikkim, Phuentsholing/Bhutan, and districts you cover.
- **Services section:** add each route as a named service ("Kolkata to Siliguri goods transport", "Transport to Bhutan", "Full truck load", "Part load") with 1–2 sentence descriptions — reuse text from `content/`.
- **Description (750 chars):** use the homepage intro paragraph from `content/homepage.md`.
- **Website:** https://hrctransport.com · **Hours:** set real hours (and mark open days accurately).
- **Photos:** upload 10+ real photos — trucks with branding, loading, warehouse/godown, office signboard, team.
  Real photos massively outperform stock. Add 2–3 new photos monthly.
- **Q&A:** seed it yourself — post the 5 FAQs from the route pages as questions and answer them from the business account.

## 3. Reviews engine (the #1 local ranking + conversion factor)
- Get your review link: GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews" → copy short URL.
- After every successful delivery, send this WhatsApp message:
  > "Thank you for choosing HRC Transport! If you were happy with the delivery, a 30-second Google review helps our small business a lot: [review link] 🙏"
- Target: 2–4 new reviews/month, steadily (a sudden burst looks fake).
- **Reply to every review** within a few days, mentioning the route naturally: "Glad your consignment reached Siliguri on time…" — replies with keywords help ranking and show buyers you're responsive.

## 4. Posts
Once a month, post a GBP update (photo + 2 lines): completed route milestones, monsoon route advisories,
new truck added, festival schedule. Keeps the profile "alive" in Google's eyes.

## 5. Citations (directory consistency)
Fix/claim these with the canonical NAP and website link:
- IndiaMART (edit existing listing name), Justdial, Sulekha, TradeIndia, ExportersIndia
- Vahak, TruckSuvidha, WheelsEye directory (transport-specific — high relevance)
- Facebook Business Page + Instagram profile with website link
Each consistent citation is a trust vote; each inconsistent one is noise.

## 6. Website ↔ GBP linking
- GBP "Website" field → homepage.
- Website footer (every page): "Find us on Google → [GBP-LINK]" + embedded Google Map of the office.
- The LocalBusiness schema in `schema/localbusiness.jsonld` ties the entity together — keep its NAP identical too.
